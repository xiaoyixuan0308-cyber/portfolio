How it works

Adoption in four steps

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Tell us about your home, household and lifestyle in your adopter profile.

  2. 2

    Find your match

    Filter by species, breed, sex and temperament to find a good fit.

  3. 3

    Apply & get checked

    We complete reference, home-suitability and pet-compatibility checks.

  4. 4

    Meet & take home

    Meet your new friend, sign the agreement and start your life together.

For shelter teams

One record for the whole journey

Staff, veterinarians and council officers each see exactly what they need — nothing more — and every change is recorded for auditing.

Animal Intake

Council officers transfer seized and stray animals with supporting documents.

Medical & Veterinary

Examinations, vaccinations, medication and medical clearance.

Behaviour Assessment

Temperament assessments, recommendations and reassessment dates.

Adoption Management

Applications, suitability checks, agreements and follow-up visits.

Role-based access for Administrators, Shelter Staff, Veterinarians, Animal Control Officers and Adopters.
