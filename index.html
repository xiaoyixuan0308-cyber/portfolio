我是肖艺宣，2027 届，求职运营 / 产品方向。我在意的不只是把事情做完，而是把事情做成：为什么要做，做到什么程度算成功，以及怎样让它不依赖某个人也能持续运转。I'm Xiao Yixuan, class of 2027, looking for operations and product roles. I care less about finishing tasks and more about getting the outcome: why something is worth doing, what success looks like, and how to make it keep running without depending on any one person.

我习惯先定义目标和衡量标准，再拆开流程找到真正的原因；问题解决后，把方法沉淀成规则或工具，让同类问题不再出现。I start by defining the goal and how to measure it, then take the process apart to find the real cause. Once a problem is solved, I turn the fix into rules or tools so the same kind of problem doesn't recur.

我也在钻研如何让 AI 产品真正落地到实际业务中。AI 能做的事很多，难的是找到值得解决的场景、说清楚效果怎么衡量，并让它稳定地融入原有流程，而不是停留在演示。日常工作中，我用 AI 快速验证想法、搭原型和处理数据；FlowPilot、PawPath 和 AI 旅行规划，都是我在这个方向上的实践。I'm also digging into how AI products actually land in real business. AI can do a lot; the hard part is finding the scenarios worth solving, defining how success is measured, and fitting it reliably into existing workflows instead of leaving it as a demo. Day to day I use AI to test ideas, prototype and handle data, and FlowPilot, PawPath and the AI Travel Planner are my hands-on work in this direction.

关键取舍Key trade-off

该如何判断优先级？How do you decide what comes first?

  • 起初我想尽量完整地还原业务规则，把预测需求、现有库存、在途数量、交付周期、经销商优先级都算进去，让结果更精确。At first I tried to model every business rule (forecast demand, stock on hand, in-transit quantities, lead times, dealer priority) to make results as precise as possible.
  • 测试中我发现，算对了不等于用户会用。系统只给出一个建议采购量，却说不清这个数是怎么来的，用户就很难信任它。Testing showed that a correct number isn't the same as a usable one. If the system gives a suggested order quantity without explaining where it came from, users won't trust it.
  • 所以我把重心转向可解释性：每个建议都能下钻查看相关的库存、需求和在途数据，并看到异常情况如何影响这个建议。So I shifted the focus to explainability: every suggestion drills down to the related stock, demand and in-transit data, and shows how exceptions affect it.
复盘Looking back

如果重做，我会怎么改What I'd do differently

  • 更早引入真实用户：先只做一个最核心的场景，比如“库存不足时怎么定补货计划”，做出最小可用版本，再根据反馈扩展其他模块，而不是先把功能做全。Bring in real users earlier: start with one core scenario, such as planning replenishment when stock runs short, ship a minimum usable version, then expand based on feedback instead of building everything first.
  • 验证“有没有用”，而不只是“算没算对”：看用户能不能快速看懂建议、据此完成决策，以及系统是否真的减少了重复分析的工作量。Validate usefulness, not just correctness: can users understand a suggestion quickly, make a decision with it, and does the system actually cut repetitive analysis?

PM/BA Panda · 从需求到原型的 Claude SkillPM/BA Panda · A Claude Skill from requirements to prototype

不是让 AI 直接写系统，而是让它先像业务分析师一样把需求挖清楚，再按 UML 一步步搭出经过验证的原型。Instead of letting AI write the system straight away, it first digs out the requirements like a business analyst, then builds a verified prototype from UML, step by step.

Skill 是给 Claude 的工作手册：一个包含流程、方法和脚本的文件夹。加载后，Claude 按规定的方法做事，而不是凭感觉发挥。A Skill is a playbook for Claude: a folder of workflow, methods and scripts. Once loaded, Claude follows the method instead of improvising.

61条验收标准，每条都有通过的测试acceptance criteria, each with a passing test
33项业务例外，逐项确认business exceptions, checked one by one

和直接让 AI 写有什么不同WHY NOT JUST ASK AI TO BUILD IT

  • 先画图，再照图写代码。5 种 UML 图按固定规则变成代码：状态机管状态流转，权限矩阵变成服务端权限，测试也照着图检查。对比测试里，直接生成的版本有至少 5 个动作放错了泳道，序列图里还出现了类图中不存在的对象，照着写就是错的权限和数据结构。Diagrams first, then code that follows them. 5 UML models become code by fixed rules: the state machine drives status changes, the permission matrix becomes server-side access control, and the tests check against the diagrams. In a side-by-side test, direct generation put at least 5 actions in the wrong swimlane and used an object in its sequence diagram that its class diagram didn't have, which would have meant wrong permissions and a wrong data structure.
  • 证明测试真的有用。测试全绿不代表能抓错。我故意埋入 21 个典型错误（规则、权限、计算、通知、界面），确认每个都被抓到；演示脚本也逐字自动排练，避免现场演示翻车（实战中就差点遇到一次）。Proof that the tests work. All-green tests don't prove they catch anything. I planted 21 typical bugs (rules, permissions, calculations, notifications, UI) and confirmed every one was caught. The demo script is also rehearsed automatically, word for word, so the live demo doesn't fall over (it nearly did once in the real run).
  • 不懂代码也能把关。每个阶段停下来，只问 3–5 个业务问题；每条规则标明出处，推测的一律请你确认；材料和制度对不上时交给你裁决，不擅自改。You can review it without reading code. Each stage stops for 3–5 business questions. Every rule names its source, and anything inferred needs your sign-off. When the material and the policy disagree, you decide; it doesn't quietly pick one.

诚实的边界HONEST LIMITS

对比测试中，没装 Skill 的 Claude 在逐行核对材料、找矛盾上反而做得更好，我据此给 Skill 补上了逐行核对。目前只在“物品借还 + 审批”一个领域完整跑通过，下一步是建业务模式库、换领域再测。In the side-by-side test, Claude without the Skill was better at checking the material row by row and spotting contradictions, so I added a row-by-row audit to the Skill. So far it has been run end to end in one domain (item lending with approvals); next come a business pattern library and tests in other domains.

贷款违约风险分析Loan default risk analysis

从原始数据到上线建议，独立完成一次完整的风控分析。A full credit-risk analysis, from raw data to a go-live recommendation, done on my own.

  • 数据Data

    发现 5,000 条记录缺失违约标签，另有"?"占位和异常值。统一清洗规则，并设计"违约率"指标，让不同人群可以直接横向比较。Found 5,000 records missing the default label, plus "?" placeholders and a malformed row. Set one cleaning rule and built a default-rate measure so groups compare directly.

  • 看板Dashboards

    两页 Power BI 看板，分别回答"哪类客户容易违约"和"哪些财务状况容易违约"，关键结论直接在图上标出。Two Power BI dashboards answering "which customers default" and "which finances default", with key findings marked on the charts.

  • 指标Metrics
    决策树Decision tree

    像一连串"是 / 否"问题，把客户一步步分组，规则直观。准确率 89.3%，但 1,061 个违约客户只认出 35 个（3.3%）：违约客户只占 11%，全判"不违约"也能拿高分。A chain of yes/no questions that splits customers step by step; easy to read. 89.3% accuracy, yet it caught only 35 of 1,061 defaulters (3.3%): with 11% defaulting, calling everyone safe already scores well.

    逻辑回归Logistic regression

    给每个因素一个权重，算出违约概率；每个权重都能解释，也能检验是否显著。准确率 85.7%，但认出 315 个违约客户（29.7%），多 9 倍。Weights each factor to estimate the probability of default; every weight can be explained and tested for significance. 85.7% accuracy, but it caught 315 defaulters (29.7%), 9× more.

    为什么选逻辑回归：对银行来说，漏掉一个违约客户的损失远大于多审核一个正常客户，所以"认出违约客户"比整体准确率更重要；而且放贷决定要能向客户和监管说清理由，逻辑回归的系数正好能解释每个因素的影响。Why logistic regression: for a bank, missing a defaulter costs far more than reviewing a safe applicant twice, so catching defaulters matters more than overall accuracy. Lending decisions also have to be explained to customers and regulators, and its coefficients show how each factor counts.

  • 上线复盘Before go-live

    识别率仍只有约 30%，需要调整阈值、处理样本不平衡后再评估。种族是显著变量之一，属于信贷中的受保护属性，上线前应剔除并做公平性检验。Recall is still ~30%, so the threshold and class imbalance need work first. Race came out significant; it is protected in lending and should be removed and the model tested for fairness before use.

我的工作习惯My working habits

  1. 01. 先理解问题，再开始行动Problem first

    遇到新任务，我习惯先了解业务背景、实际需求和限制条件，再决定使用什么工具和方法。When I take on something new, I first learn the business context, real needs and constraints, then choose the tools and methods.

    案例：微创供应链 POC 需求梳理Example: Requirements for the Wicresoft supply-chain POC

  2. 02. 把复杂任务拆成可执行的步骤Structured execution

    对复杂任务先明确优先级、关键节点和交付标准，避免陷入不重要的细节。For complex work I set priorities, milestones and a definition of done first, so I don’t get lost in details that don’t matter.

    案例：FlowPilot Lab：拆成风险发现、月度计划、周度履约、查询追溯 4 个环节Example: FlowPilot Lab, split into risk detection, monthly planning, weekly fulfilment and traceability

  3. 03. 不只追求完成，还要验证结果Validate before trusting

    不轻易接受第一次得到的结果，尤其关注数据准确性、边界情况和异常场景。I don’t accept the first result at face value, and look hardest at data accuracy, edge cases and exceptions.

    案例：PawPath 的 22 项越权访问检查Example: PawPath’s 22 access-control checks

  4. 04. 主动沟通，尊重不同观点Communicate with clarity

    在团队合作中重视目标一致和信息透明。面对不同意见，先理解对方的依据，再讨论解决方案。In a team I care about shared goals and open information. When views differ, I understand the other side’s reasoning before we discuss solutions.

    案例：GEE 国际商业竞赛跨国团队协作Example: Cross-border teamwork in the GEE competition

  5. 05. 接受反馈，持续迭代Iterate and improve

    不把第一版方案当作最终答案，而是通过测试和反馈不断调整，让成果逐步接近实际需求。The first version is never the final answer; testing and feedback keep moving the result closer to what people actually need.

    案例：供应链预测逻辑优化、PawPath 原型迭代Example: Supply-chain forecast logic fix, PawPath prototype iterations

  6. 06. 保持学习，并将知识用于实践Learn by building

    面对陌生领域，习惯通过文档、实践和测试建立理解，再将学到的知识转化为具体成果。In an unfamiliar field I build understanding through docs, hands-on work and testing, then turn it into something concrete.

    案例：从会计背景转向信息系统与 AI 应用开发Example: From accounting to information systems and AI application development
