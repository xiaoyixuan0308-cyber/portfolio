你好，我是肖艺宣Hi, I'm Yixuan Xiao

我是肖艺宣，2027 届，求职运营 / 产品方向。我在意的不只是把事情做完，而是把事情做成：为什么要做，做到什么程度算成功，以及怎样让它不依赖某个人也能持续运转。I'm Xiao Yixuan, class of 2027, looking for operations and product roles. I care less about finishing tasks and more about getting the outcome: why something is worth doing, what success looks like, and how to make it keep running without depending on any one person.

我习惯先定义目标和衡量标准，再拆开流程找到真正的原因；问题解决后，把方法沉淀成规则或工具，让同类问题不再出现。I start by defining the goal and how to measure it, then take the process apart to find the real cause. Once a problem is solved, I turn the fix into rules or tools so the same kind of problem doesn't recur.

我也在钻研如何让 AI 产品真正落地到实际业务中。AI 能做的事很多，难的是找到值得解决的场景、说清楚效果怎么衡量，并让它稳定地融入原有流程，而不是停留在演示。日常工作中，我用 AI 快速验证想法、搭原型和处理数据；FlowPilot、PawPath 和 AI 旅行规划，都是我在这个方向上的实践。I'm also digging into how AI products actually land in real business. AI can do a lot; the hard part is finding the scenarios worth solving, defining how success is measured, and fitting it reliably into existing workflows instead of leaving it as a demo. Day to day I use AI to test ideas, prototype and handle data, and FlowPilot, PawPath and the AI Travel Planner are my hands-on work in this direction.

关键取舍Key trade-off

该如何判断优先级？How do you decide what comes first?

  • 起初我想尽量完整地还原业务规则，把预测需求、现有库存、在途数量、交付周期、经销商优先级都算进去，让结果更精确。At first I tried to model every business rule (forecast demand, stock on hand, in-transit quantities, lead times, dealer priority) to make results as precise as possible.
  • 测试中我发现，算对了不等于用户会用。系统只给出一个建议采购量，却说不清这个数是怎么来的，用户就很难信任它。Testing showed that a correct number isn't the same as a usable one. If the system gives a suggested order quantity without explaining where it came from, users won't trust it.
  • 所以我把重心转向可解释性：每个建议都能下钻查看相关的库存、需求和在途数据，并看到异常情况如何影响这个建议。So I shifted the focus to explainability: every suggestion drills down to the related stock, demand and in-transit data, and shows how exceptions affect it.
复盘Looking back

如果重做，我会怎么改What I'd do differently

  • 更早引入真实用户：先只做一个最核心的场景，比如“库存不足时怎么定补货计划”，做出最小可用版本，再根据反馈扩展其他模块，而不是先把功能做全。Bring in real users earlier: start with one core scenario, such as planning replenishment when stock runs short, ship a minimum usable version, then expand based on feedback instead of building everything first.
  • 验证“有没有用”，而不只是“算没算对”：看用户能不能快速看懂建议、据此完成决策，以及系统是否真的减少了重复分析的工作量。Validate usefulness, not just correctness: can users understand a suggestion quickly, make a decision with it, and does the system actually cut repetitive analysis?

一个住址，串起约 98 万美元的舞弊链One address, a US$980K fraud scheme

我用 SQL 检查一家公司的付款、工资和客户记录，查出一套持续多年、约 98 万美元的内部舞弊，并给出止损和长期防范方案。I used SQL to examine a company's payments, payroll and customer records, uncovered a multi-year internal fraud worth about US$980K, and proposed how to stop it and keep it from happening again.

约 98 万~$980K美元舞弊资金in fraud
10家假供应商，收款约 79 万fake vendors, ~$790K paid
5名假员工，年薪约 16 万ghost employees, ~$160K a year
1,119笔付款全部低于审批线payments, all under the limit

项目背景CONTEXT

一家从燃油发电转型 EV 充电的企业，委托评估 IT 治理、内部控制和舞弊风险。A company moving from fuel generators to EV charging asked for a review of its IT governance, internal controls and fraud risk.

体现的能力WHAT IT SHOWS

  • 在大量数据里找到关键线索：假供应商、假员工、假客户都登记在同一个住址，顺着这条线串起三类舞弊。Finding the thread: fake vendors, ghost employees and a fake customer shared one home address, which linked three schemes.
  • 从数字规律里发现问题：最高一笔 9,969 美元，全部"刚好"低于审批线，说明有人系统性地拆单绕过审批。Reading the pattern: every payment sat just under the limit, a sign of deliberate splitting to avoid approval.
  • 追到根本原因：没有审批权的前台人员批准了 143 笔付款，财务负责人给自己的工资审批了 104 次。Root cause: a receptionist with no approval rights approved 143 payments, and the CFO approved her own pay 104 times.
  • 把一次检查变成长期机制：6 条检查规则做成季度自动监控，异常直接报董事会。From one-off to ongoing: 6 checks turned into quarterly automated monitoring reported straight to the board.
  • 用数字支撑决策：每条结论附金额和笔数，支撑"冻结权限、外部调查"的建议。Numbers that drive decisions: every finding carries amounts and counts to back an immediate access freeze and external investigation.

我的工作习惯My working habits

  1. 01. 先理解问题，再开始行动Problem first

    遇到新任务，我习惯先了解业务背景、实际需求和限制条件，再决定使用什么工具和方法。When I take on something new, I first learn the business context, real needs and constraints, then choose the tools and methods.

    案例：微创供应链 POC 需求梳理Example: Requirements for the Wicresoft supply-chain POC

  2. 02. 把复杂任务拆成可执行的步骤Structured execution

    对复杂任务先明确优先级、关键节点和交付标准，避免陷入不重要的细节。For complex work I set priorities, milestones and a definition of done first, so I don’t get lost in details that don’t matter.

    案例：FlowPilot Lab：拆成风险发现、月度计划、周度履约、查询追溯 4 个环节Example: FlowPilot Lab, split into risk detection, monthly planning, weekly fulfilment and traceability

  3. 03. 不只追求完成，还要验证结果Validate before trusting

    不轻易接受第一次得到的结果，尤其关注数据准确性、边界情况和异常场景。I don’t accept the first result at face value, and look hardest at data accuracy, edge cases and exceptions.

    案例：PawPath 的 22 项越权访问检查Example: PawPath’s 22 access-control checks

  4. 04. 主动沟通，尊重不同观点Communicate with clarity

    在团队合作中重视目标一致和信息透明。面对不同意见，先理解对方的依据，再讨论解决方案。In a team I care about shared goals and open information. When views differ, I understand the other side’s reasoning before we discuss solutions.

    案例：GEE 国际商业竞赛跨国团队协作Example: Cross-border teamwork in the GEE competition

  5. 05. 接受反馈，持续迭代Iterate and improve

    不把第一版方案当作最终答案，而是通过测试和反馈不断调整，让成果逐步接近实际需求。The first version is never the final answer; testing and feedback keep moving the result closer to what people actually need.

    案例：供应链预测逻辑优化、PawPath 原型迭代Example: Supply-chain forecast logic fix, PawPath prototype iterations

  6. 06. 保持学习，并将知识用于实践Learn by building

    面对陌生领域，习惯通过文档、实践和测试建立理解，再将学到的知识转化为具体成果。In an unfamiliar field I build understanding through docs, hands-on work and testing, then turn it into something concrete.

    案例：从会计背景转向信息系统与 AI 应用开发Example: From accounting to information systems and AI application development
